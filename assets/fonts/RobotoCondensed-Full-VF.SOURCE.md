# Full Roboto Condensed font source

Downloaded 2026-11-09 from the official Google Fonts repository:

- Font: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/google/fonts/main/ofl/robotocondensed/RobotoCondensed%5Bwght%5D.ttf
- License: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/google/fonts/main/ofl/robotocondensed/OFL.txt

The font is retained byte-for-byte, renamed locally for an unambiguous full-family path. SHA-256: `dace262afcee68a5276f200d8026c57221735c0118ab5fda8c2c0d3dc409a8d0`.

The accompanying `RobotoCondensed-Full-VF.LICENSE.md` preserves the complete upstream SIL Open Font License 1.1 and copyright text. License SHA-256: `0e4cc6ece88573545be2ed25835363662a6182ba4a4c1b5c8feda52add30e8a6`.

Unlike the legacy Latin subset at `RobotoCondensed-Regular.ttf`, this source contains Cyrillic. Native Godot `Font.has_char` checks cover English, Russian including both forms of yo, and German umlauts/sharp s with operating-system fallback disabled. Keep legacy files until their consumers are deliberately migrated.
