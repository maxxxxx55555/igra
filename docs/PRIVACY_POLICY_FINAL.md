# Release privacy policy

Publish the English and Russian text in [the store policy](../store/privacy-policy-template.md) for the Wi-Fi bonus release. The contact address in both versions belongs to the developer.

The Android permissions are in `export_presets.cfg`. The source audit is in [PRIVACY_AUDIT.md](PRIVACY_AUDIT.md), and the store declarations are in [IARC_ANSWERS.md](../store/IARC_ANSWERS.md).

## Children

The game collects no personal data from children or adults. The store's target audience and estimated rating are recorded in the store declarations.
