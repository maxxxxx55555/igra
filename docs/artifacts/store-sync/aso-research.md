# ASO research record — store-sync pass (2026-09-13)

Sources consulted (web, 2026-09-13):

1. **Google Play ASO guides (2026):**
   - Asolytics "Google Play App Optimization: The Complete ASO Guide (2026)" — title ≤30
     chars with 1 primary term, no forced phrasing; short ≤80 with 1–2 natural keywords;
     benefit-first; "ensure it reflects real app functionality to prevent negative user
     signals" (= the anti-vaporware rule Google itself warns about).
   - AppLaunchFlow "Google Play Store Optimization & ASO Guide 2026" — Play indexes title +
     short + full text (no hidden keyword field); main term + natural variations a handful of
     times in the 4,000-char long description, "written for humans, not crammed"; asset order:
     icon → short → first screenshots.
   - Yellowhead "Google Play ASO" — weighting order title > short > long; frequency AND
     density, never spammy.
2. **Competitor listings sampled (top-of-category survival/horror/puzzle mobile):**
   - *The Face Horror Game* (Play) — opens with a blackout scene, uses "blackout",
     "flashlight", "offline", feature bullets as short benefit lines, story hook first.
   - *Flashlight Tag: Ghosts Escape* (Play) — "The Flashlight is Your Only Friend / light
     also draws unwanted attention / battery management + sight-vs-stealth balance" — the
     exact duality our listing already sells; kept our stronger, concrete wording.
   - *Zombie Hunter: Offline Games* (Play) — "STORY DRIVEN" and "OFFLINE" as header-carrying
     terms; lesson: offline/story-driven earn real placement when claimed truthfully.
   - Uptodown "Survival Horror for Android" category — puzzle/hide/escape/resource-management
     phrasing dominates; supports weaving "survival puzzle" (not just "horror") for discovery.
