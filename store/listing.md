## Full description

### EN

```
BLACKOUT CITY. YOU ARE THE GRID ENGINEER.

The "Architect Project" disaster plunged the city into permanent night.
You are a survivor with one flashlight, a fading battery, and a job no
one else is left to do: get the power grid running again, district by
district, street by street.

EVERY DISTRICT YOU RESTORE STAYS LIT
Eleven districts make up one city, and you light it one district at a
time: pick the next one on the city map and travel there. The contrast
between the black streets you have not reached and the ones you have
already saved is the whole shape of the game. Light is the reward, not
just a tool.

THE DARK IS REALLY DARK
An unpowered district is pitch black: no glowing windows, no helpful
haze. You walk it by your flashlight and by sound, and a monster you
have not caught in your beam or under a lamp stays unseen: no marker,
no name, no crosshair cue.

A MAP THAT ONLY KNOWS WHAT YOU LIT
Your map draws streets and buildings, and only where a lamp you
restored lit them and you saw them. It never shows a monster, an item
or even you: every battery, part and secret has to be found by
exploring.

WHAT'S INSIDE
- 11 districts, relit one at a time and reached from the city map
- 11 enemy types — ten hunters and the Architect — each with its own senses, weaknesses, and behaviour
- 5 endings, decided by how much of the city — and of the truth — you recover
- 2 fully localized languages (English, Russian)
- A 5-layer adaptive music system that shifts with the danger around you
- Stealth built on noise and visibility, not a detection meter
- Crafting and a workbench for upgrading your one real tool: the flashlight
- Checksum-verified saves with automatic backup recovery
- 58 daily challenges, 31 achievements, and a New Game+ after the credits

The flashlight is simultaneously your only weapon against some of what
hunts you, your only way to see, and the thing that gives you away every
time you switch it on.

CONTROLS
Touch: a virtual joystick and a button for every action (fire, reload,
melee, UV, dodge, flashlight, inventory, crafting, map), plus a
double-tap dodge.
PC: WASD and mouse — same game, same save file; export or import it
between devices, no account, no server.

THE WHOLE CITY ON DAY ONE
All 11 districts ship complete — story, lore and endings included. No
paywalled content, no season pass. Fully offline: this blackout is a
survival game, and the story is yours to drive.

Made with AI help: part of the music (Google Lyria), the painted menu, screen backdrops, city map and district cards, some of the in-game textures and the key art of this store page are AI-generated.
```

## Data-safety notes (confirmed against the shipped RC FINAL v2 build, 2026-09-13)
- Offline-first, no account system, no analytics SDK in the current build.
- No personal data collected or shared by the game.
- No multiplayer ships in the launch build: the LAN prototype
  (`scripts/multiplayer/`, archived per `docs/A10_multiplayer_decision.md`)
  is unreachable from any UI, and the game itself opens no network
  connections. (Play Console Data-Safety answers must say "no data
  collected, no users" — an earlier draft line about LAN multiplayer was
  removed here as unbacked.)
- Ads: the optional AppLovin MAX plugin ships with **no SDK key configured**,
  so no real ad ever loads and nothing is collected; until a key is set the
  ad slots show an offline debug placeholder popup only. If a real key is
  enabled before submission, the Data-Safety form and privacy policy §5 must
  be updated first — see `docs/store/HUMAN_CHECKLIST.md`.
- The Android build declares `INTERNET` (reserved for the optional ad plugin)
  and `VIBRATE` (touch haptics) only — see `export_presets.cfg`.
- Privacy-policy URL placeholder — see `store/privacy-policy-template.md`.
